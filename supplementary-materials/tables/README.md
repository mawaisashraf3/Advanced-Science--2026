# Supporting tables

The UTF-8 CSV tables compile evidence from the published literature. Each file preserves the supplied review table, including its original units and source-specific qualifications.

| File | Contents | Reading the values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Table 1](Table_1.csv) | Nine synthesis or post-treatment routes for MXenes and checks for hydrogel integration. | Process duration and temperature refer to the stated operation. Include washing, delamination, and recovered usable material when comparing throughput. |
| [Table 2](Table_2.csv) | Nine capacitive, hybrid, and battery microcell case studies or comparators. The fourth column records the reported location and measurement basis. | Areal and volumetric capacitance, energy, and power use different denominators. Read F cm⁻², F cm⁻³, µWh cm⁻², mWh cm⁻³, mW cm⁻², mW cm⁻³, and mAh cm⁻² within the stated rate, geometry, and voltage window. Geometry can refer to the active region, wet stack, or whole footprint, depending on the source. |
| [Table 3](Table_3.csv) | Seven comparisons of reported results with idealized bounds or application targets. | The constant-capacitance energy expression and fixed-resistance peak-power bound have stated assumptions. Zinc stoichiometric capacity uses zinc mass, while the printed cell uses footprint. The 54.8% energy retention is a within-study rate comparison. Fatigue, recovery, and constituent fate have separate test endpoints. |

Use the [numbered bibliography](../references/bibliography.md) to resolve the bracketed references. In Table 3, the two source entries marked **[177]** under damage recovery and end-of-life degradation refer to the degradable epidermal hydrogel paper by Li et al. at DOI 10.1021/acsnano.1c01751. It is [reference 175 in the manuscript](../references/bibliography.md#ref-175). The CSV retains its original citation numbers.

The comparisons report published values and the review's analytical bounds. Primary measurement records remain with the cited studies.
