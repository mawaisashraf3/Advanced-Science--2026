# Supplementary materials

- [Figure guide](figures/README.md) covers all 20 manuscript figures and links to the original schematics, available source crops, and panel-by-panel explanations.
- [Supporting tables](tables/README.md) describes the three supplied CSV files, their units, their study-level limitations, and a legacy citation crosswalk.
- [References](references/README.md) links to the manuscript-numbered bibliography and the citation-manager export.
- [Published panel provenance](figures/panel-provenance.csv) records image attribution, crop changes, and license terms by asset.

The resource supports a literature review. The tabulated measurements come from the cited studies. Uptake models, comparative bounds, and schematic plots are identified separately from observed results. Computational methods are discussed in the review context. The source collection supplies no analysis scripts, model inputs, or primary instrument records.
